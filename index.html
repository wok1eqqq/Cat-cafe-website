Cat Cafe

Booking Information

Each booking gives you 1 full hour in the café. During your visit, you will have free and unlimited access to our hot and cold drinks stations, including tea, coffee, and soft drinks.

Bookings run for one hour from your selected time on the calendar below, so please make sure you arrive on time to enjoy your full session.

Important Information

If you are planning to visit with children, or if you have any questions about the café, house rules, or cat welfare, please check our FAQs section before making a booking.

We look forward to welcoming you to Cat Café for a relaxing and memorable experience.
