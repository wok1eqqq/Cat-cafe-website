Booking Information

We understand that visitors may have questions before booking, so we have provided some helpful information below. If you still need assistance, you are always welcome to contact us for more support.

We recommend booking in advance, as spaces in the café are limited. This helps us keep the environment calm and comfortable for both our guests and our cats. Each visit lasts for one hour from the time you select when booking, so it is important to arrive on time to enjoy your full session.

The price of your visit includes unlimited access to our self-service drinks station, where you can enjoy a variety of hot and cold drinks during your stay. This allows you to relax and enjoy your time without any extra charges.

Children are welcome in the café; however, they must be supervised at all times. We advise checking our rules before booking to make sure the café environment is suitable for younger visitors. To protect our cats and maintain a safe space, outside pets are not allowed in the café.

The wellbeing of our cats is always our top priority. All of our cats are well cared for and have access to quiet areas where they can rest. They are never forced to interact with guests, and we ask visitors to respect their space at all times by being calm and gentle.

If you arrive late to your booking, your session time may be reduced, as we are unable to extend bookings due to other scheduled visits. For information about cancelling or changing a booking, please refer to our booking policy or contact us directly.

We hope this information helps you prepare for your visit, and we look forward to welcoming you to Cat Café.
